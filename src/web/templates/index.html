Road Damage Classification Scheme

Ultralytics YOLO11 model trained on the Road Damage Dataset (RDD)

D00 Severity: Moderate

Longitudinal Crack

Cracks running parallel to the direction of vehicular travel. Primarily caused by paving lane joint shrinkage or subgrade settlement.

Color Code: #008CFF Azure
D10 Severity: Moderate

Transverse Crack

Cracks running perpendicular to the roadway centerline. Typically caused by thermal contraction cycles and daily temperature swings.

Color Code: #32CD32 Lime Green
D20 Severity: High

Alligator Crack

Interconnected series of multi-sided cracks resembling reptile skin. Indicates structural fatigue failure of the asphalt base under heavy traffic loads.

Color Code: #FFA500 Bright Orange
D40 Severity: Critical

Pothole

Bowl-shaped depressions in the road surface resulting from subsurface water penetration and traffic wear. Poses severe hazards to vehicles.

Color Code: #DC2222 Crimson Red
